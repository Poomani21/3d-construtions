03 / Studio

PRECISION IS
A CULTURE,
NOT A CLAIM.

Meridian was founded in 2005 by engineers who were tired of drawings that could not be built. Two decades later we operate as one team across design, engineering and site — which is why our tolerances hold and our programmes land.

We take on work where geometry is difficult, sites are tight and the client expects the model and the building to be indistinguishable.

  • Design-build, single point of responsibility
  • In-house structural & facade engineering
  • Model-first delivery with clash-free coordination
